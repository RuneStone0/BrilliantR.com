# Market research: small coaching businesses in Utah

First target vertical to research for [website-optimization](overview.md): solo/1-2 person coaching businesses (life, business, career, health, relationship coaching). Rationale to validate: coaches typically aren't technical, often built their own site years ago on a template, and rely on their website for credibility/lead-gen — a plausible fit for a website-optimization pitch.

## Bonus finds: dead domains

While researching, ran into three coaching businesses whose domains don't resolve at all (DNS failure, not just a broken page) — despite still showing up in search results and directory listings (Yelp, Alignable, Google):

- **Summit Business Coaching LLC** (Steve Potter, Lehi, UT) — summitbusinesscoachingllc.com is dead, **but the user pointed out the business may now be at [summitbusinesscoaching.net](https://summitbusinesscoaching.net/), which is live.** Caveat: the content there doesn't name Steve Potter or Lehi — it's framed as a "senior coach only," fully remote, nationwide practice with no location given. Plausibly a rebrand/repositioning of the same person, but not confirmed — worth a quick call before treating it as a match. Cloudflare-hosted; no CMS fingerprint detected (possibly custom-built or Cloudflare Pages), so the "dead domain" rescue narrative doesn't apply to this one if it *is* the same business — it'd instead be a story about an old domain quietly going dark while a newer one took over.
- **Victor Fralof Coaching** (American Fork, UT) — victor-fralof.com
- **Replace and Recover** (Brett Morris, Logan, UT) — replaceandrecover.com

The latter two are still the strongest "website rescue" leads: an active local business currently invisible online because their domain lapsed or their host disappeared. Worth a closer look (confirm they're still operating, e.g. via phone/social) before treating any of these as leads.
