A model built around your results, not my hours.

For AI automation projects, I use a shared savings model. Here's how it works.

We define the saving

We identify a specific, measurable cost — a software subscription, a manual workflow, a service you're paying for — and agree on the annual value in writing before any work starts.

I quote 50% of that saving

My fee is exactly 50% of the agreed annual saving. If the project saves you $10,000 a year, my fee is $5,000. No surprises.

You pay upfront, I start building

Payment is made before the project begins. This keeps both parties committed and removes any ambiguity about what success looks like.

You get a working solution

Deployed on your own cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud or AWS). You own it. You control it. Running costs are paid directly by you — I have no incentive to over-engineer.

Not every project fits the ROI sharing model. I also offer hourly consulting at $150/hr and a monthly retainer of $1,000/month for 10 hours. New clients get their first 10 hours at $120/hr.
